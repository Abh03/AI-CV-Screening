Test Automation Engineer
Technical role | Engineering and delivery

TEAM AND CONTEXT
Join a cross-functional retail fulfilment team supporting business-critical services. The role includes design reviews,
delivery and a shared support rotation.
RESPONSIBILITIES
Develop reliable browser and API regression suites for checkout and fulfilment.
Design test data, investigate flaky checks and report reproducible defects.
Integrate smoke packs into CI and assess release risk with product teams.
REQUIRED TECHNICAL SKILLS
Selenium or Playwright or Cypress
API Testing or Postman
SQL
JUnit or TestNG or Python or Java
PREFERRED SKILLS
Jenkins
Docker
Jira
EXPERIENCE
At least 2 years of relevant professional experience. Evidence of implementation and operational ownership is
expected.
EDUCATION
Degree in a relevant technical discipline or equivalent practical experience; no degree filter.
SELECTION CRITERIA
Each required group needs at least one listed alternative. Preferred tools and domain experience are ranking
